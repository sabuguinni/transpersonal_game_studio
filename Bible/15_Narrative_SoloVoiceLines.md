# Solo Voice Lines — Spoken Aloud, To No One
### Short barks triggered by gameplay events. No narrator, no NPC replies.

Rule: he only speaks when a human alone would actually speak — first sighting
of a new threat, a near-death escape, a genuine discovery. Not every action
gets a line. Silence is the default state.

## First sighting (per species, once)
- Triceratops: "Big. Not interested in me. Good."
- Parasaurolophus: "...that's the sound. That's really the sound."
- Ankylosaurus: "You're not even going to look at me, are you."
- Velociraptor: "No. No no — back up. Rock face. Now."
- Tsintaosaurus: "New one. Never seen that crest shape in any restoration."

## Near-death escape (random pool, any predator)
- "Okay. Okay. Still breathing."
- "That's twice. I don't get a third."
- "Note to self — LOUDLY — don't do that again."
- "Heart's still going. That's the only good news."

## Successful craft / tool made
- "Sharp enough. It'll do."
- "Improvised. Not proud of it. It'll do."
- "That's a spear now. Wasn't, ten minutes ago."

## Low health / injured
- "Not good. Not good, keep moving."
- "Bleeding slows me down more than it kills me. Move."

## Discovery of gem fragment / lore object
- "That doesn't belong in this rock layer. That's — that's not natural."
- "Same material as the first one. Has to be."

## Domestication milestone (creature stops fleeing/attacking on sight)
- "You remember me. You actually remember me."
- "Easy. Easy — good. That's new."

## Environmental / weather
- "Storm's coming. Herd already knows."
- "Cold's setting in. Wrong direction if I'm reading this terrain right —
  that's toward the high ground."

## The Return (late-game, approaching Snowy Rockside)
- "Colder every day. This is the right direction."
- "If the pattern holds, it's here. It has to be here."
- "Second fragment. Then I find out if any of this works."
